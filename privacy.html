Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 13, 2026

What the prototype stores

StudyNest’s current browser prototype stores tasks, notes, flashcards, study points, and learning-path progress in your browser’s local storage on the device you use. This prototype does not provide accounts or cloud syncing.

Affiliate links

If affiliate links are added in the future, the linked merchant or affiliate program may use cookies or similar tracking to attribute eligible purchases. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit their services. Affiliate links will be labeled.

Children and sensitive information

Do not enter passwords, sensitive personal information, or information about other people into StudyNest. The project is intended to provide study tools; it is not designed to collect student profiles.

Contact

A public contact email has not yet been configured for this prototype. Add a contact method before using this as a full public service.
